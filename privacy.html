Introduction

We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use the Aimee app, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Swiss law.

Personal information

Personal information refers to any data that can identify an individual, including:

  • Name
  • Birth date
  • Gender
  • Country
  • Email
  • Phone number

Sensitive personal information

Aimee connects to your Aimee device over Bluetooth and processes health-related measurement information in accordance with the law. By providing consent, you agree that we can collect, transfer, store, and process this data. This information is used to provide personalized services and support.

App permissions

  • Bluetooth, to connect and communicate with your Aimee device
  • Face ID or device passcode, if you enable app lock

Aggregated data

Aggregated data refers to statistical or demographic data derived from personal data. This data is not considered personal data as it does not directly or indirectly reveal your identity. We use aggregated data to analyze trends and improve our services.

Child data privacy

Our services are not intended for use by minors (children under 13). Any children's data collected is not done knowingly and it is the responsibility of the user. We take special care to protect the privacy of children.

Data collection and use

We collect data in the following ways:

  • Explicit collection: information you provide directly to us through registration and in-app forms. This data is used to respond to queries, provide support, send updates, and operate your account.
  • Automatic collection: information collected automatically, such as user language, operating system, and device identifiers needed to run the app. This data helps us improve our services.

We always use the least amount of data necessary to provide services to you.

Data sharing with third parties

All data is stored, transferred to, and processed encrypted in Microsoft Azure cloud in Switzerland. Without your explicit consent, your data is not shared with third parties. Special occasions might require sharing your data with third parties (for example support or device servicing), and all shared data must meet the same security levels as in our company.

Our services may contain links to third-party services that may not adhere to our data privacy standards. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

Secure data storage

All communication and data storage are encrypted and meet strict security rules. It is the user's responsibility to use a strong and unique password or other sign-in methods. We take all necessary measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

All your personal data is stored in Switzerland. Transferring your personal data to different jurisdictions requires your consent. All personal information is stored only for the necessary period to fulfill its purposes.

Data access and deletion

You can request a data export (all customer records) by contacting our data privacy office. You will not pay any fee unless your request is considered excessive. You also have the right to be forgotten. All your data can be deleted upon your request made to our data privacy office. Certain data will be stored even after we delete your personal data where legally required (for example financial transaction data).

A limited number of employees have access to your data. All employees with access have proper data privacy training and hold liability for their actions.

Data privacy office

You can contact our data privacy office via email or by mail using the contact information listed at the beginning of this policy. We make our best effort to respond within one month to reasonable requests.

Updates to this privacy policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy at any time. You will be notified by email if you have registered with our services. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
